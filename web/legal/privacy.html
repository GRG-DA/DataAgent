1. Who we are

[[COMPANY_NAME]] ("we", "us") operates a hosted analytics service built on the DataAgent application ("the Service"). The Service lets an organisation upload its own business data, query it with SQL, ask natural-language questions of it and publish dashboards and reports from the results.

When you create an account, [[COMPANY_NAME]] is normally the controller of the personal data described in this policy, and our hosting, email and model providers act as our sub-processors. Where we process data on behalf of an organisation that has contracted with us, that organisation is the controller and we are the processor; the terms of that processing are in the Data Processing Addendum.

2. What we process

We process three kinds of data. We do not sell any of it, and we do not use customer content for advertising.

2.1 Account data

Your email address, display name, password hash, role, organisation and tenant membership. If you sign in through an identity provider, we also receive the identifier and the profile attributes that provider chooses to release.

2.2 Customer content

The files you upload (spreadsheets, CSV and Parquet files, JSON) and the tables they become. This is the business data you ask us to analyse. We store it in a DuckDB warehouse, in a dedicated schema per tenant (analytics_t_<tenant>), so that one tenant's tables are physically separate from another's. We also store the queries you run, the dashboards you build, the conversations you have with the assistant, and the reports you export.

2.3 Operational data

Request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, path, status code), audit records of security-relevant actions, error traces, and usage counters. These records are used to operate, secure and improve the Service.

2.4 What we deliberately do not collect

  • No advertising or cross-context behavioural identifiers.
  • No payment card numbers. Payments are handled by a separate billing service that we never see.
  • No contents of your private repositories, mailboxes or devices beyond what you explicitly upload to the Service.

3. Where the data lives

The Service is hosted in [[DATA_REGION]]. Your warehouse, your raw uploads and the identity store are held on infrastructure operated by [[SUBPROCESSOR_HOSTING]], with object storage for uploads provided by [[SUBPROCESSOR_STORAGE]]. Raw uploads are addressed by an opaque tenant identifier, never by a customer name.

Data at rest is encrypted by the infrastructure provider. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. The encryption keys used by the application itself (the session-signing key and the entitlement-signing key) are stored separately from the data and are never included in a backup unless an operator explicitly asks for it.

4. Artificial intelligence processing

Read this before you enable the assistant. When you ask a question, the Service builds a prompt from your question and, where relevant, from schema metadata and query results. That prompt is sent to the model provider configured for your deployment.

The model provider is [[SUBPROCESSOR_LLM]]. We send the minimum context needed to answer the question: table and column names, and in most cases aggregate results rather than row-level data. We do not send your account password, your API keys, or other tenants' data. Where your deployment is configured for a local or self-hosted model, the request never leaves the deployment.

Model providers are instructed not to use your content to train their models. Prompts and responses are not retained by us beyond the operational logs described in section 5.

5. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or an equivalent regime applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the Service and answer support requests.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and understand aggregate usage. We balance these against your rights and expectations.
  • Legal obligation — to keep audit records where the law requires it.
  • Consent — for optional marketing email, and where required for a feature that is not necessary to run the Service.

8. Your rights

Subject to the law that applies to you, you can ask us to:

  • Access — a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and confirmation of whether we hold it.
  • Rectification — correction of anything inaccurate.
  • Erasure — deletion of your account and the data attached to it. You can also delete your account yourself from the account settings; deletion cascades to your tables, dashboards, conversations, API keys and notification preferences.
  • Restriction and objection — pause processing that you rely on for a lawful interest, or object to it entirely.
  • Portability — your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. Every table can be exported as CSV, Parquet or Excel from the dashboard, and the full account export is available on request from [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, for any processing that relies on it.
  • Complain — to the supervisory authority in [[JURISDICTION]].

We answer within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we release anything. We do not charge for a copy.

9. Security

Access to customer content is restricted by role and, for administrative operations, by tenant. Every tenant is isolated in its own schema and its own raw-upload directory. Passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes. Sessions are signed and expire. Roles are manageable over the API and every role change is audited. Uploads are checked by content, not only by file extension, and are limited in size and type.

We run a documented vulnerability-management programme and a security overview that describes the controls in more detail.
