## Global Rules

1. **Primary CTA:** “Free Windows download + 14-day Pro trial, no card.”
2. **Link:** `https://data-agent.me/download` (Instagram: “link in bio”).
3. **Export truth:** `.pbix` export/import is Pro. The 14-day trial unlocks it. Never say “free `.pbix`.”
4. **Free tier truth:** 25 AI requests/day, 3 dashboards, 7 days of history. Never say “unlimited.”
5. **Privacy wording:** “Your data stays on your machine.” Do not say “100% private,” “zero telemetry,” absolute privacy claims (“never leaves your machine,” “fully offline”), or “no internet ever.”
6. **Platform:** Windows 10/11 x64 only. Say it in every install-related post.
7. **Size:** “about 440 MB” for the installer; “about 4.7 GB” for the local model.
8. **Open source:** do not use the phrase until the source is actually published. The site claim was removed on 2026-10-26; see `docs/decisions/ADR-021-private-source-until-published.md`.
9. **AI disclosure:** if the video uses an AI-generated presenter, add: “AI-generated presenter; product demo is real screen capture.”
10. **Hashtags:** 5–8 targeted tags. Never 30. Rotate the sets below.
11. **One CTA per post.** Do not stack “download,” “subscribe,” “comment,” and “buy” in the same caption.
12. **No fake urgency.** No “only today,” no countdowns, no “last chance.”

## Do Not Use

- “Free `.pbix` export” — it is Pro/trial.
- “Unlimited AI” — caps are 25/50/100 per day.
- “100% private” / “zero telemetry” — use “your data stays on your machine.”
- “Open source” — until the source is published.
- “Works on Mac/Linux” — Windows only.
- “Replaces Power BI” — it exports to Power BI.
- “No internet needed” — install and model pull need internet.
- “GDPR/HIPAA compliant” — no audit evidence.
- “AI does everything” — the analyst still reviews.
- 30 hashtags — use 5–8.
