# Free B-Roll Sources and Shot List

**Rule:** the product is the proof. B-roll is seasoning — never let it replace the real screen recording.

---

## Free, commercial-use sources

| Source | Best for | Licence check |
|---|---|---|
| **Pexels** (pexels.com/videos) | Laptop, desk, typing, coffee, office | Pexels Licence; no attribution required |
| **Pixabay** (pixabay.com/videos) | Abstract data, city, workspace | Pixabay Content Licence |
| **Mixkit** (mixkit.co) | Short tech/office clips | Mixkit Licence; check per clip |
| **Coverr** (coverr.co) | Clean workspace/desk shots | Coverr Licence |
| **Videvo** (videvo.net) | Keyboard, screen, office | Check per clip (some require attribution) |

Download 1080p or higher. Avoid clips with visible brand logos, readable client data, or identifiable faces you do not have permission to use.

## 10-second B-roll recipe

| Time | Shot | Use |
|---:|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Macro: messy printed spreadsheet / CSV on screen | Hook texture |
| 0:02–0:04 | Hand on mouse, laptop screen glow | Transition |
| 0:04–0:06 | Keyboard typing, shallow depth of field | Cover a cut |
| 0:06–0:08 | Coffee cup beside laptop, morning light | Human context |
| 0:08–0:10 | Abstract bar/line chart animation | Chart transition |

Use 1–2 clips maximum per 21–22-second short. Keep them under the captions, never over the SQL/DAX explanation.

---

## Editing rules

- Grade to match the screen recording: neutral, slightly cool, low saturation.
- Add a subtle 5–10% dark overlay so captions stay readable.
- Do not use stock footage of a different analytics product's UI.
- Do not imply the stock person is the founder or a customer.
- No music-driven montage that hides the product.
